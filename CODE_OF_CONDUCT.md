# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent.
- Focus on technical issues and reproducible facts.
- Avoid harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or moderate contributions that violate this code.
Repeated or severe violations may lead to temporary or permanent bans from project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues privately to project maintainers.
